==============================================================================
       AGGREGATE FOREIGN ASSET VALUE vs the Rs 20,00,000 s.43 threshold       
==============================================================================

Black Money Act s.43 penalty: Rs 10,00,000 per assessment year for
an omitted Schedule FA. The proviso inserted w.e.f. 1-10-2024 disapplies it where
the aggregate value of foreign assets other than immovable property does not
exceed Rs 20,00,000.

The proviso fixes no valuation date for non-bank assets, so both a peak-during-
the-year and a closing-at-31-December basis are shown. Neither is settled law.

This report aggregates EVERY non-immovable holding in the ledger. On the better
view the proviso aggregates only the UNDISCLOSED assets, so for a year whose
Schedule FA was partly completed the figure that matters is lower than the one
below, and this tool cannot know which holdings were disclosed. An UNDER verdict
is safe on either reading; treat an OVER verdict for such a year as a prompt to
compute the undisclosed subset by hand. See docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md issue 5.

Both figures are taken from rules/AY2027-28.json, verified
2026-08-03. Sources are in that file; docs/ANNUAL-REVIEW.md says what to
re-check. Neither figure is computed or remembered.

==============================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS
==============================================================================

- 'Sum of per-holding peaks' adds each holding's own best day. Holdings do not
  all peak on the same day, so this is at or above any single-day total. It is
  the conservative direction for a threshold test.
- Cash is included only for accounts listed in cash_balances.csv. Any account
  missing from that file is counted on its securities alone and is understated.
- Values are rupees, converted at the SBI TT buying rate, per the Schedule FA
  rule. Every figure traces to the per-lot CSV written alongside this report.
- Every figure is reconstructed from the taxpayer's own broker statements only.

Peak basis used for the Schedule FA rows themselves: usd
Generated 2026-08-24 22:44:00 by itr-prep.

Arithmetic, not advice. Where a year straddles the line, or is close to it,
have a professional confirm the valuation basis before relying on the verdict.
